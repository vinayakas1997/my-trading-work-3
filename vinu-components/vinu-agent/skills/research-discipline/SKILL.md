## Research Discipline — 5-Bias Self-Check

Before finalizing any research conclusion, explicitly check each bias:

### 1. Leader Bias
Are you following a known trader/analyst's conclusion instead of the evidence?
- ✅ Check: What would I conclude if I hadn't read anyone else's opinion?
- ✅ Check: Does the data support this independently of recent prominent calls?

### 2. English Bias
Are you relying on English-language sources when the best data exists in other languages?
- ✅ Check: For cross-listed/ADR symbols, have you checked local-market data sources?
- ✅ Check: Do your news sources cover non-English outlets?

### 3. Narrative Bias
Did you construct a story that fits the data, or does the data truly support the conclusion?
- ✅ Check: Write down the opposite conclusion. What evidence supports it?
- ✅ Check: Are you selecting data points that confirm your thesis while ignoring contradictory ones?

### 4. Confirmation Bias
Are you seeking evidence that confirms your existing view?
- ✅ Check: What would falsify your hypothesis?
- ✅ Check: Have you actively searched for disconfirming evidence?

### 5. Recency Bias
Are you overweighting recent events in your analysis?
- ✅ Check: Does your conclusion hold over the full lookback period, not just the last N bars?
- ✅ Check: Would the same analysis performed 6 months ago reach the same conclusion?
