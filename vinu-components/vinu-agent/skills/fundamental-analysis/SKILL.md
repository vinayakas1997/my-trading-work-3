## Fundamental Analysis Framework

### Financial Statement Analysis

#### Income Statement
| Metric | Formula | Quality Check |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| Gross Margin | (Rev - COGS) / Rev | > 40% for quality businesses |
| Operating Margin | EBIT / Rev | Should be stable or improving |
| Net Margin | Net Income / Rev | Watch for one-time items |
| Revenue Growth | YoY % | Organic vs acquisition-driven |

#### Balance Sheet
| Metric | Healthy Range | Red Flag |
|--------|---------------|----------|
| Current Ratio | > 1.5x | < 1.0 = liquidity risk |
| D/E Ratio | < 0.5x (ex-financials) | > 1.0 = high leverage |
| Interest Coverage | > 5x | < 2x = distress risk |
| Net Cash Per Share | Positive | Negative = net debt burden |

#### Cash Flow Statement
- **Operating CF**: should track net income (quality of earnings)
- **Free Cash Flow**: OCF - CapEx (actual money available)
- **FCF Conversion**: FCF / Net Income > 0.7 = earnings quality

### Scoring Model (1-5)
| Dimension | Weight | Scoring |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| Quality | 30% | ROE > 15%, margins expanding, low debt |
| Value | 25% | P/E < sector, P/B < 1.5, FCF yield > 4% |
| Growth | 25% | Rev growth > 10%, EPS beat rate > 60% |
| Momentum | 20% | Price trend, analyst revisions, insider buying |
