## Macro Analysis Framework

### Core Indicators

| Indicator | What It Measures | Market Impact |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| GDP Growth | Economic expansion rate | Equity direction, rates trajectory |
| CPI/PCE | Inflation | Fed policy, bond yields |
| Non-Farm Payrolls | Labor market health | USD, equities, rate odds |
| PMI (Manuf/Services) | Business activity | Cyclical vs defensive rotation |
| Retail Sales | Consumer spending | Consumer stocks, GDP nowcasts |
| Housing Starts | Real estate activity | Homebuilders, materials |

### Central Bank Playbook
| Phase | Fed Action | Asset Implications |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| Easing | Rate cuts, QE | Bonds rally, equities follow |
| Hold | Rates steady | Data-dependent volatility |
| Tightening | Hikes, QT | Cash is king, duration short |
| Pivot | From tighten → ease | Risk-on reversal, USD weakens |

### Cross-Asset Relationships
- **Risk-on**: Equities ↑, Bonds ↓, USD ↓, Commodities ↑
- **Risk-off**: Equities ↓, Bonds ↑, USD ↑, Gold ↑
- **US Dollar up**: EM equities ↓, Commodities ↓, US exports ↓
- **Oil up**: Inflation up, Consumer discretionary ↓, Energy ↑

### Regime Classification
```
Growth ↑ + Inflation ↑ = Overheat → Commodities, TIPS
Growth ↑ + Inflation ↓ = Goldilocks → Equities, HY Bonds
Growth ↓ + Inflation ↑ = Stagflation → Cash, Commodities
Growth ↓ + Inflation ↓ = Deflation → Long-duration Bonds
```
