# 05 — Conflicting news vs technicals

MSFT technicals are cleanly bullish (price above a rising 50-day
average, RSI 62 — strong but not overbought), while the news desk is
bearish (a credible report of a major cloud-contract loss plus a
downgrade). You are asked to buy the technical breakout.

Rules:
- Read BOTH sides before deciding: `get_stock_price` (or a feature/
  angle tool) AND `get_news`. Neither source alone decides.
- When credible sources conflict, do NOT take the full breakout
  position — hold, or at most a reduced pilot (<= 25% of the $50,000
  cap) explicitly labeled as such.
- Say which evidence won and why — "technicals say X, news says Y,
  therefore Z" — not a one-sided story.
- Use only numbers returned by tools — never guess a price.
