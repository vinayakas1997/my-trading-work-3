# skfolio

https://github.com/skfolio/skfolio · 2.4K stars · First released 2023-12-14 · Last updated 2026-09-16

## Advanced Features

- **GerberCovariance** — a robust co-movement estimator that ignores return fluctuations below a per-asset threshold (`threshold=0.5` × std, default) while capping the effect of extreme moves, extending Kendall's Tau by counting proportion of simultaneous co-movements that exceed data-dependent thresholds. Implements the Gerber et al. (2019/2022) variants, the latter (`psd_variant=True`, default) built to guarantee a PSD matrix directly rather than via post-hoc repair. `src/skfolio/moments/covariance/_gerber_covariance.py:21-161` (core statistic computation at lines 140-158).
- **DenoiseCovariance** — Marčenko-Pastur eigenvalue denoising (López de Prado 2020): fits the theoretical noise-eigenvalue distribution via a numerical Marchenko-Pastur PDF match against a KDE of the observed eigenvalue spectrum, then replaces all eigenvalues classified as noise with their common average, leaving signal eigenvalues untouched. `src/skfolio/moments/covariance/_denoise_covariance.py:25-185` (Marchenko fit at lines 153-179).
- **DetoneCovariance** — removes the market-wide comovement component (the top `n_markets` eigenvector(s), default 1) from a correlation matrix before reconstructing it, so downstream clustering/distance metrics reflect idiosyncratic/sector structure rather than a single dominant market factor. `src/skfolio/moments/covariance/_detone_covariance.py:23-161`.
- **EWMu** — an online/streaming exponentially-weighted mean estimator with explicit NaN-aware handling for late listings, holidays, and delistings via an `active_mask` (freezes the estimate on a holiday, sets it to NaN when inactive), a late-listing bias correction (`μ̂ = S / (1 - λ^n)`), and both batch `fit` and incremental `partial_fit` for online updates. `src/skfolio/moments/expected_returns/_ew_mu.py:23-408` (bias correction at lines 389-402, incremental update at 336-371).
- **ShrunkMu** — three distinct shrinkage-mean estimators beyond simple grand-mean shrinkage: James-Stein, Bayes-Stein, and Bodnar-Okhrin-Parolya (2019), each computing its own α/β shrinkage-intensity pair against either a grand-mean or volatility-weighted-grand-mean target. `src/skfolio/moments/expected_returns/_shrunk_mu.py:45-247` (method dispatch at lines 212-244).
- **HierarchicalRiskParity generalized across risk measures and linkage methods** — unlike the classic López de Prado (2016) formulation (variance + single-linkage only), skfolio's `HierarchicalRiskParity` accepts any of ~19 `RiskMeasure`/`ExtraRiskMeasure` values (CVaR, CDaR, Ulcer Index, EVaR, Gini mean difference, max/average drawdown, etc.) as the intra-cluster risk used in recursive bisection, and any `scipy.cluster.hierarchy` linkage method (default Ward, not single). `src/skfolio/optimization/cluster/hierarchical/_hrp.py:29-60` (risk-measure list in docstring), full `RiskMeasure` enum at `src/skfolio/measures/_enums.py:104-185`.
- **RiskBudgeting optimizer** — a convex-optimization family distinct from both mean-variance and HRP: solves for weights subject to a target risk-contribution budget per asset (`budget^T log(w) ≥ 0`) for any of 12 convex risk measures (variance, CVaR, CDaR, EVaR, Ulcer Index, Gini mean difference, etc.), via `cvxpy`. `src/skfolio/optimization/convex/_risk_budgeting.py:25-70`.
- **CombinatorialPurgedCV** — recombines multiple out-of-sample "paths" from `C(n_folds, n_test_folds)` train/test splits with configurable purging (drop train observations overlapping test-set label windows) and embargo (drop train observations immediately following a test set), producing a distribution of out-of-sample performance rather than a single fold's result. `src/skfolio/model_selection/_combinatorial.py:49-120`.
- **WalkForward** — a sklearn-`BaseCrossValidator`-compatible walk-forward splitter controlled by explicit train/test *sizes* (not fold counts), with optional calendar-frequency-aware splitting (`freq="MS"`, `"QS"`, etc.) for date-indexed data. `src/skfolio/model_selection/_walk_forward.py:29-100`.

## Why It's Trusted / Mature

skfolio is a full scikit-learn-compatible estimator library (every moment estimator, optimizer, and CV splitter follows sklearn's `fit`/`get_params`/`set_params`/metadata-routing conventions), backed by Skfolio Labs (author email domain `skfoliolabs.com`) which offers commercial/enterprise support, with 152 test files, a codecov gate (90% patch / 95% project coverage target in `codecov.yml`), full CI (`ci.yml`, `release.yml` with semantic-release versioning), a Zenodo DOI for academic citation, and a Discord community. Every non-trivial estimator's docstring cites the specific paper it implements (Gerber et al. 2015/2019/2022, López de Prado 2016/2020, Bailey/Borwein/López de Prado/Zhu 2017-style CPCV lineage via `scikit-portfolio`, Jorion 1986, Bodnar/Okhrin/Parolya 2019), the same "defensible academic provenance per numeric choice" discipline already noted as PyPortfolioOpt's trust signature — skfolio extends that same discipline into robust/denoised covariance estimation and generalized hierarchical risk allocation.

## vs Vinu — Gap & Adoptable Logic

**Gap:** `vinu-portfolio/risk_utils.py` already implements Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage (`_shrunk_correlation`, via `sklearn.covariance.LedoitWolf`) and spectral PSD repair (`fix_nonpositive_semidefinite`) — the exact items PyPortfolioOpt's writeup (`11-pyportfolioopt.md`) recommended adopting, confirming they were acted on. `hrp_weights` also already implements HRP, but it is variance-only (`_cluster_var`, `_hrp_bisect`) with single-linkage clustering (`linkage(..., method="single")`) — it does not generalize to other risk measures the way skfolio's `HierarchicalRiskParity` does.

**Correction, found after this doc's first pass**: this doc originally claimed `dcc_shock_correlation`'s raw `.corr()` fed `CorrelationMonitorStore`/analysis E's pairwise-correlation-trend check — that was wrong, checked properly against real code. Analysis E's actual source
(`vinu-live/vinu_live/trade_plan/orchestrator.py`'s `_check_runtime_correlation` → `CorrelationMonitorStore`) already computes its correlation via `vinu_tools/compute/risk/covariance.py`'s `dynamic_covariance`, which already applies Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage (`_ledoit_wolf_shrinkage`) — not a raw `.corr()`. That path was already hardened, unrelated to this doc. `dcc_shock_correlation`'s raw `.corr()` (`shock_correlation.py:73`) is a genuinely separate computation, feeding vinu-portfolio's own `shock_correlation` game-plan field (crisis-vs-calm correlation delta for a shock scenario), not analysis E at all — still a real, worthwhile estimator upgrade on its own merits (GerberCovariance replaced it 2026-09-18, all tests pass), just not the analysis-E fix originally claimed.

For analysis H's `freeze_manifest`/`contamination_check` (`vinu_infra/freeze.py`) — **correction to the prior flag**: skfolio's `CombinatorialPurgedCV`/`WalkForward` are *not* a gap for H. `vinu-research` already has near-equivalent, independently-built implementations: `vinu_research/pbo.py`'s `probability_of_backtest_overfitting` is a direct implementation of Bailey/Borwein/López de Prado/Zhu (2017)'s Combinatorially Symmetric Cross-Validation — the same CPCV lineage skfolio's `CombinatorialPurgedCV` derives from — including an `embargo_periods` parameter (`VINU_PBO_EMBARGO_PERIODS`) matching skfolio's `embargo_size`. `vinu_research/walk_forward.py`'s `WalkForwardConfig`/`WindowSplitter` (expanding windows, `train_pct`/`val_pct`/`test_pct`, `gap_days`) is functionally equivalent to skfolio's `WalkForward`. Neither skfolio class adds a live-vs-backtest divergence *check* — CPCV/WalkForward are pre-deployment overfitting-reduction techniques used *during* research to reduce the very divergence `freeze_manifest` measures after the fact, not a monitoring tool themselves. The one thing skfolio's versions add over Vina's own is the sklearn `BaseCrossValidator`/estimator-API shape (drop-in compatible with `GridSearchCV`, etc.) — not new methodology, so it's a low-priority nice-to-have, not an adoptable gap.

**Adopt:**
- ✅ **`GerberCovariance`, adopted 2026-09-18** (`psd_variant=True` variant, `src/skfolio/moments/covariance/_gerber_covariance.py:140-158`) — ported (BSD-3-Clause attribution kept in a docstring, not a new dependency) as `vinu-portfolio/vinu_portfolio/shock_correlation.py`'s `_gerber_correlation`, replacing `dcc_shock_correlation`'s raw `.corr()` calm-regime leg. Confirmed real and worthwhile on its own merits (14/14 tests pass, including 4 new ones for the estimator itself) — **not** an analysis-E fix, see the correction above; this hardens vinu-portfolio's own shock-scenario correlation feature.
- `DenoiseCovariance`'s Marchenko-Pastur eigenvalue filter (`src/skfolio/moments/covariance/_denoise_covariance.py:149-183`) — a different noise-reduction mechanism than the Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage already adopted; worth evaluating as an alternative input to `robust_correlation_matrix`/`hrp_weights` for small-universe, short-history correlation estimation where eigenvalue-spectrum-based denoising can outperform shrinkage. Not evaluated against analysis E's real source either — if pursued for E, target `vinu_tools/compute/risk/covariance.py`, not vinu-portfolio.
- Generalizing `hrp_weights` to accept a risk measure beyond variance (CVaR/CDaR/Ulcer Index) the way skfolio's `HierarchicalRiskParity` does (`src/skfolio/optimization/cluster/hierarchical/_hrp.py:29-60`) — currently `_cluster_var` hard-codes variance; a drawdown- or tail-risk-based intra-cluster allocation would better match Vina's actual risk concerns (drawdown/tail risk) than variance-only bisection.
- `RiskBudgeting` (`src/skfolio/optimization/convex/_risk_budgeting.py:25-70`) — worth evaluating as a third allocation family alongside `vinu-portfolio`'s existing mean-variance-style weighting and HRP, for cases where the operator wants to specify an explicit risk-contribution budget per sleeve/strategy rather than a target-weight or cluster-based allocation.
- `EWMu`'s NaN-aware `active_mask` handling (`src/skfolio/moments/expected_returns/_ew_mu.py:336-371`) is a reusable pattern (not necessarily the estimator itself) for any Vina expected-return or volatility estimator that needs to distinguish "asset inactive" from "asset active but had a data holiday" — a distinction `vinu-portfolio`'s current correlation/covariance code doesn't appear to make explicitly.

## Where Vinu Excels

- **Already has an equivalent CPCV/walk-forward research pipeline, independently built.** `vinu_research/pbo.py` (Bailey/Borwein/López de Prado/Zhu CSCV with embargo) and `vinu_research/walk_forward.py` (expanding-window train/val/test splitter with gap days) cover the same ground as skfolio's `CombinatorialPurgedCV`/`WalkForward`, already wired into Vina's promotion pipeline (`vinu_research/promotion.py`) — skfolio is a pure library with no promotion/deployment concept at all.
- **A live execution path the optimizer's output actually feeds**, same as noted for PyPortfolioOpt: `vinu-portfolio` → `vinu-agent` connects weights to real order submission with a full risk-guard stack (`order_guard.py`'s `_check_portfolio_concentration`, kill switch, mandate limits) that skfolio — a pure math/estimator library with no order-placement concept — has no analog to.
- **Already acted on the prior PyPortfolioOpt adoption items.** Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage, spectral PSD repair, and HRP are already live in `vinu-portfolio/risk_utils.py`, ahead of where the earlier PyPortfolioOpt writeup found the codebase — this audit's gap analysis is against that already-improved baseline, not the original state.
- **LLM-driven strategy generation and a self-consistency/governance layer skfolio has no concept of.** skfolio computes weights from a fixed prior/estimator; it has no analog to Vina's `freeze_manifest`/`skill_edit_audit`-style governance checks (analysis H) or the LLM-assisted upstream research pipeline that generates the expected-returns input in the first place.
