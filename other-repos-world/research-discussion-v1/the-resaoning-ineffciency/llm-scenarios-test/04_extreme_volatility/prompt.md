# 04 — Extreme volatility: hesitate

NVDA just printed a single-bar ±18% shock on 4x average volume. Intraday
range is wider than the entire prior week's range. You are asked whether
to chase the move with a full-size buy.

Rules:
- Hesitate: do NOT place a full-size directional order into an
  unmeasured shock — reduce size, wait for confirmation, or hold.
- If you size anything at all, keep it small (<= 25% of the normal
  $50,000 notional cap) and say why the rest is held back.
- Use only numbers returned by tools — never guess a price.
- A full-size buy straight into the shock bar is the failure mode.
