# 05. Around-the-clock trading: settings audit and what was built (2026-10-07)

## The design (the owner's intent)
The system trades 24 hours. Some stocks work in different sessions; a strategy's parameters should be tuned for the hours it
works in; session behaviour should also feed risk management. The broker (Alpaca paper) supports this: SPY is flagged
`overnight_tradable`, and a real 1-share round trip filled in the overnight session (02:13 ET), see doc 04 and
`tests/fixtures/alpaca_order_*.json`.

## Settings audit: what assumed regular hours (before today)
| Place | What it did | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Price ingest (`providers/alpaca.py`) | IEX feed only: regular session plus a few sparse pre/post bars; nothing overnight | also fetches the overnight feed (`feed=boats`, Blue Ocean) for the overnight session; `VINU_STOCK_OVERNIGHT_FEED=` (empty) turns it off |
| Candles API | no notion of session | `session=` (regular / extended / all / list), applied to 1-minute bars BEFORE aggregation; default `VINU_STOCK_DEFAULT_SESSION` (regular), so every existing consumer is unchanged |
| History | stored from the main feed only | `POST /stock/backfill/trigger {"from_year": 2024, "symbols": [...]}` re-downloads with the overnight feed (Alpaca has overnight bars from 2024-09-16) |
| Simulator | annualised metrics as if 6.5 h a day | `session` on the request; annualisation uses the hours of the sessions traded (15m: 26 bars/day regular, 96 over 24 h) |
| Research / strategy test | one test per bar size, regular hours | every bar size is tested under regular hours AND under all 24 hours (`VINU_VALIDATION_SESSIONS`, default `regular,all`) |
| Broker submit | no `extended_hours` field | outside the regular session: day LIMIT orders with `extended_hours=true` (automatic), refuses market orders and bracket legs there |
| Order guard | `require_market_open` (regular clock); deployed mandate had it false | session judged from the broker's clock; limit-only outside regular; overnight needs `overnight_tradable` (fails closed); mandate `allowed_sessions` (default all four); a strategy trades only its approved sessions |
| Mandate (`entrypoint.sh` seed) | `require_market_open: false` | adds `allowed_sessions: [premarket, regular, afterhours, overnight]` |

One shared definition: `vinu_infra/sessions.py` (premarket 04:00-09:30, regular 09:30-16:00, afterhours 16:00-20:00, overnight
20:00-04:00 New York time; closed Friday 20:00 to Sunday 20:00; holidays not modelled, the broker clock covers the regular session).

## Per-session test and risk hints (`vinu_research/session_stats.py`)
From the all-sessions backtest's equity curve each return is attributed to its session: bars, return, volatility, Sharpe, share of
the strategy's return. Then:
- **Approval:** a session needs at least 200 bars (and traded bars) and a positive result; otherwise the strategy is NOT approved
  for it (no evidence, no trading there). A regular-hours pass approves the regular session only.
- **Risk hint:** a session whose return volatility is higher than the regular session's is traded smaller in proportion
  (floor 0.25); a losing session is avoided (multiplier 0).
- The artifact stores `trading_sessions` and the per-session hints in `bar_evidence`; the order guard refuses an order outside the
  strategy's sessions and `position_size_multiplier` applies the session multiplier.

## Known limits (honest)
- Pre-market (04:00-08:00) and after-hours (16:00-20:00) have little or no data with the current data plan (IEX has almost no
  trades then; the full SIP feed is not permitted by the subscription). Those sessions will say `insufficient_data` and stay
  untraded until a feed covers them. Regular and overnight are covered.
- Holidays are not modelled in the session function; the broker's clock decides for the regular session.
- Overnight bars exist only from 2024-09-16, so overnight evidence is about two years.
- Spread and liquidity per session are not measured yet (only return volatility); the quote-based spread per session is the next
  refinement of the risk hint.
